About

Hi, I’m Kyara.

I'm an artist based in Your City. I work mostly in paint and ink, and I'm drawn to small everyday moments — the light on a kitchen table, houseplants, people waiting for the bus.

I started drawing as a kid and never really stopped. These days I split my time between personal work, commissions, and filling far too many sketchbooks.

If you'd like to work together or just say hi, my inbox is always open.
